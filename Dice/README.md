# Your Brain Is The Product

Alternative name: **The Psychology of Being Human**  
Arabic concept: **من اللودو لحد السوشيال ميديا.. مين بيتحكم في سلوكك؟**

An educational short-form series about neuroscience, behavioral psychology, gamification, game design, business, Product Management, religion, and ethics. It connects everyday behavior to scientific concepts and real product decisions.

## Series identity

- Language: Egyptian Arabic with English technical terminology
- Tone: conversational, story-driven, curious, lightly humorous, and non-academic
- Audience: Product builders, PMs, founders, developers, students, and general audiences interested in behavior
- Platforms: Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
- Cadence: one episode per week for 12 weeks
- Target duration: 45–75 seconds
- Publication dates: not assigned; manual approval required

## Content pillars

1. Religion & Ethics
2. Neuroscience
3. Behavioral Psychology & Gamification
4. Business & Product Design

## Learning sequence

- Episodes 02–04 form the scientific and behavioral foundation for Episodes 05–10.
- Each episode depends on the previous episode for publishing order.
- Episodes 05–10 should not be finalized until Episodes 02–04 pass research review.
- Episode 12 is the synthesis and ethical conclusion.
