# Episode 01 — هو ليه اللودو ممكن تكون حرام؟

- English title: Is Playing Ludo Actually Haram?
- Series: Your Brain Is The Product
- Pillars: Religion & Ethics + Neuroscience
- Workflow status: Idea Backlog

- Platforms: Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts

## Content Strategy

- Main idea: Separate the Islamic legal discussion about dice from the scientific explanation of uncertainty and reward.
- Why it matters: People often collapse Hadith, gambling, dice, and neuroscience into one oversimplified claim.
- Learning objective: Understand that scholarly rulings require attribution, while reward science explains behavior rather than religious law.
- Key message: اسأل الأول: النص بيقول إيه؟ العلماء فهموه إزاي؟ والعلم بيفسر السلوك إزاي؟
- Intended reaction: “I need to distinguish the ruling, its interpretation, and the behavioral mechanism.”

## Hook

- Opening question: "صاحبك اللي رفض يلعب لودو.. هل عنده وجهة نظر؟"
- Alternative hook: "هل أي لعبة فيها نرد تبقى قمار؟ الإجابة محتاجة نفصل 3 حاجات."
- Visual opening: Dice roll freezes before landing; three labels appear: Hadith / Fiqh / Behavior.

## Research & Fact Checking

- Core concepts: Hadith concerning dice; Sahih Muslim 2260; scholarly differences; dice versus gambling; randomness; uncertainty; reward anticipation.
- Scientific terminology: uncertainty, reward anticipation, reward prediction, reinforcement.
- Primary sources: Verify the Hadith in the relevant Sahih Muslim numbering editions; record Arabic text, translation, grading, and context.
- Religious research: Collect attributed opinions from recognized scholars or schools and identify disagreement explicitly.
- Scientific research: Review reliable work on reward anticipation and uncertainty; do not imply neuroscience proves a ruling.
- Claims requiring verification: Exact Hadith wording/numbering; scope of each legal opinion; whether wagering is present in the example.
- Potential misconceptions: “All dice games are gambling”; “randomness is automatically addictive”; “brain science determines halal and haram.”

## Script Development

- Opening: Use the main hook.
- Storytelling: Two friends disagree before a Ludo game.
- Explanation: Introduce the Hadith, then say its legal application has scholarly interpretation that must be attributed. Separately explain that uncertainty can affect reward anticipation.
- Real-world example: Ludo without wagering versus a game with money at stake.
- Business application: Random reward mechanics require ethical review, transparency, and attention to user vulnerability.
- Conclusion: النص، الحكم الفقهي، والتفسير العلمي 3 مستويات مختلفين.
- CTA: "تحب الحلقة الجاية نفهم إزاي المكافأة بتعلّمنا نكرر السلوك؟"

## Production

- Setup: Direct-to-camera with physical dice used only as a visual prop.
- B-roll: Ludo board, non-monetary play, source cards with citations after verification.
- Animation: Three-column distinction; uncertainty-to-anticipation diagram.
- On-screen text: “Hadith ≠ interpretation ≠ neuroscience” and “الخلاف يُنسب لأصحابه”.
- Editing notes: Do not display a ruling as universal; add verified source details before recording.
