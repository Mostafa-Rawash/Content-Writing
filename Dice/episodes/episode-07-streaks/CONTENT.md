# Episode 07 — سر الـStreak والـDaily Login

- English title: Why Do We Care About Streaks?
- Pillar: Business & Product Design
- Workflow status: Idea Backlog
- Publish date: TBD
- Target duration: 45–75 seconds

## Content Strategy

- Main idea: Streaks combine progress visibility, commitment, and the prospect of losing an accumulated record.
- Learning objective: Explain loss aversion, goal-gradient effects, habit formation, and progress tracking without presenting one universal explanation.
- Key message: الـstreak ممكن يدعم عادة مفيدة، أو يخلّيك تحافظ على الرقم بعد ما الهدف يختفي.

## Hook

- Opening question: "ليه ممكن ترجع تفتح تطبيق مخصوص علشان متخسرش سلسلة أيامك؟"
- Alternative hook: "إنت محافظ على العادة، ولا محافظ على الرقم؟"
- Visual opening: Streak counter at 29; finger hesitates over “continue.”

## Research & Fact Checking

- Concepts: loss aversion, goal-gradient effect, habit formation, progress tracking, commitment and consistency.
- Sources: Original and review literature for each construct; product-specific evidence where claims are made.
- Claims requiring verification: Whether streak loss is best explained by loss aversion in the chosen example; conditions for goal-gradient effects.
- Misconceptions: Streaks automatically form habits; every user experiences loss aversion equally; a longer streak proves learning.

## Script Development

- Example: Language learning or fitness tracking.
- Business application: Streaks can support retention and consistency when recovery options and user goals are respected.
- Ethical note: Offer grace days, pause controls, and non-punitive recovery where appropriate.
- Conclusion: الـstreak أداة؛ قيمته في السلوك المفيد اللي وراه، مش الرقم نفسه.
- CTA: "طيب إيه الفرق بين reward معروفة وrandom reward؟"

## Production

- B-roll: Generic calendar and progress counter.
- Animation: Progress increases; perceived distance to goal shrinks; lost-streak recovery path.
- On-screen text: “Habit ≠ streak” and “Support, don’t punish”.
- Editing notes: Avoid claiming loss aversion is the sole mechanism.

## Record-Ready Video

### TOV

Confessional and lightly comic at first, then analytical. Treat streaks as a design tool with trade-offs, not a villain. Emphasize the question “habit or number?”
