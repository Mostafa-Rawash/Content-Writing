# Episode 12 — هل من حق الـProduct Manager يلعب في سلوك المستخدم؟

- English title: Does a Product Manager Have the Right to Influence User Behavior?
- Pillars: Religion & Ethics + Business & Product Design
- Workflow status: Idea Backlog
- Publish date: TBD
- Target duration: 45–75 seconds

## Content Strategy

- Main idea: Product teams inevitably shape choices, so their responsibility is to make influence transparent, proportionate, user-centered, and aligned with sustainable value.
- Learning objective: Connect neuroscience, behavioral design, gamification, user autonomy, and responsible Product Management.
- Key message: القدرة على زيادة الرجوع مش تصريح باستخدام أي mechanic؛ الـPM مسؤول عن القيمة والاختيار والضرر المحتمل.

## Hook

- Opening question: "لو عرفت إزاي تخلي المستخدم يرجع كل يوم.. هل لازم تستخدم القدرة دي؟"
- Alternative hook: "كل design بيأثر في قرار—بس إمتى التأثير يبقى مسؤول؟"
- Visual opening: PM dashboard asks “Daily active users ↑”; second screen asks “User outcome?”

## Research & Fact Checking

- Concepts: ethical product design, behavioral design, user-centered design, sustainable engagement, responsible PM, user autonomy.
- Sources: HCI/product ethics research, official guidance where relevant, and the verified scientific sources used across Episodes 02–11.
- Claims requiring verification: Ethical framework terminology, regulatory statements, and any real product example.
- Misconceptions: Neutral design exists; all influence is manipulation; growth and ethics are necessarily opposites.

## Script Development

- Story: A PM discovers a mechanic that increases daily return but may not improve user outcomes.
- Explanation: Use a decision test—user goal, transparency, control, proportionality, vulnerable users, long-term consequence.
- Business application: Pair engagement metrics with outcome, trust, complaint, opt-out, and harm indicators.
- Conclusion: اسأل مش بس “نقدر نخليه يرجع؟” اسأل “رجوعه أفاده؟ وكان عنده اختيار حقيقي؟”
- CTA: "أي mechanic في السلسلة شايفها مفيدة، وأي واحدة محتاجة حدود؟"

## Production

- Animation: Ethical design checklist with green/red decision path.
- On-screen text: “Value / Autonomy / Transparency / Sustainability”.
- Editing notes: Close the series by referencing Episodes 01–11; avoid presenting the checklist as a universal legal standard.
