# Mostafa OS

Personal operating system for **2026-11-13 through 2027-02-12**.

## Purpose

Mostafa OS is the control system for daily life and the next 90 days. It is designed to:

- establish a reliable waking and shutdown routine;
- run a focused, measurable job search;
- maintain content-publishing commitments without duplicating production work;
- complete a 12-week Technical Product Management curriculum;
- finish one deliberately selected primary course;
- review progress every week and make monthly course corrections.

## Daily Operating Rhythm

### Morning

1. Get out of bed without opening social media.
2. Water, wash, prayer or quiet reflection.
3. Five minutes of movement and daylight.
4. Review the day's Top 3.
5. Begin the first focused block before reactive work.

### During the day

1. Capture new obligations in **Inbox**.
2. Work from **This Week**, not from memory.
3. Protect one focused block for the week's primary career or learning outcome.
4. Do not create duplicate content-production tasks in Mostafa OS.

### Evening

1. Clear loose notes into **Inbox**.
2. Select tomorrow's Top 3.
3. Prepare the desk, clothes, and necessary materials.
4. Place the phone away from the bed.
5. Record bedtime and one lesson from the day.

## Weekly Rhythm

| Day | Primary focus |
|---|---|
| Sunday | Weekly review, Inbox processing, and weekly commitments |
| Monday | Job applications and target-company research |

| Wednesday | Networking, follow-ups, and interview preparation |
| Thursday | TPM study completion and course progress |
| Friday | Content commitment and personal administration |
| Saturday | Deep work, catch-up, and recovery |

The rhythm is a default, not a rigid rule. Preserve the weekly outcomes when dates move.

## Weekly Review Checklist

1. What moved forward?
2. What remained stuck, and why?
3. How consistent was the morning routine?
4. How many applications, outreaches, replies, calls, and interviews occurred?
5. What content was published?
6. What TPM study and course work was completed?
7. What health or personal-admin work needs attention?
8. What was the biggest distraction or energy drain?
9. Is the Inbox empty?
10. What are next week's Top 3?
11. What commitment should be removed?

## System Rules

1. Capture first; organize during reviews.
2. Every active task needs a clear next action.
3. Keep **This Week** deliberately small.
4. Use quality applications instead of indiscriminate volume.
5. Use only verified career claims in applications, interviews, and content.
6. Maintain one canonical record for content production in **Content-Writing**.
7. Study one primary course at a time.
8. When a task is repeatedly postponed, simplify, delegate, schedule, or delete it.
9. Weekly reviews are mandatory; plans without reviews become stale lists.
10. Consistency is measured across weeks, not by one imperfect day.
